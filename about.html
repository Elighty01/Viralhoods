About Viralhoods

Viralhoods is a quick way to see what is trending in the world right now. We cover politics, finance, health, entertainment, research, science, AI, tech, sports and world news.

How it works

Each day our bot reads public news feeds from trusted publishers. It writes a short summary in its own words and links to the original story. We never copy full articles. Open the source to read the whole story.
